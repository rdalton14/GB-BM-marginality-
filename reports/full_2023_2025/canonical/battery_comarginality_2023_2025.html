Battery co-marginality: batteries frequently share the margin with other batteries

This diagnostic tests whether a battery appearing in a tied marginal set is accompanied by another battery or by a different technology.

Main result. After applying the explicit battery override register, 1,985 of 10,682 battery-marginal SPs (18.6%) contain more than one marginal BMU. Conditional on those tied sets, 80.1% contain another battery, while 42.1% contain another technology. These events overlap when a set contains multiple batteries and another technology.

Interpretation. Battery co-marginality is clustered within technology, but the figure does not demonstrate strategic bidding competition. It records common marginal-price attribution among accepted actions at the same final price.

Classification robustness

The source register leaves several named BESS assets in OTHER. The adjusted specification changes only the BMUs listed in the explicit override register and preserves the original label for audit.
