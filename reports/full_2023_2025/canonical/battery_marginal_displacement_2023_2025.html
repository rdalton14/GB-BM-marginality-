Battery marginal-action displacement, 2023-2025

This report separates a descriptive accepted-stack comparison from a static BOD replacement counterfactual. Positive gaps mean that the non-battery comparator is more expensive to the System Operator than the battery action.

Coverage

11,479 battery unit-period observations across 9,267 unique battery-marginal SPs.

BOD counterfactual currently covers 9,267 of 9,267 target SPs (100.0%). The report is safe to regenerate while the checkpointed BOD collection expands.

Interpretation boundary. The accepted-stack result is only a price-distance diagnostic. The BOD result holds the balancing requirement and submitted ladder fixed, replaces the battery MWh mechanically, and assumes national merit-order availability. It does not reproduce NESO network constraints, behavioural rebidding, operational feasibility, or the complete PAR/flagging System Price calculation.

2. BOD replacement counterfactual

The battery's accepted MWh is filled from unused classified non-battery BOD actions in the residual tail beyond the observed battery margin: offers at or above the battery price, ordered low to high, and bids at or below it, ordered high to low. The last action required sets the counterfactual action price. Original prices are used on both sides because unaccepted BOD actions have no settlement-adjusted final price.

The prices underneath the gap

These are raw submitted action prices. On bid-active periods, the gap calculation reverses the sign so that a positive gap still means a more expensive replacement to NESO.

Methodological notes

  • Offer prices are used directly; bid prices are multiplied by −1 to express both sides as cost to the System Operator.
  • Primary results are leave-one-battery-unit-out. Each tied battery BMU therefore forms one observation; sp_weight gives every SP total weight one.
  • Submitted BOD MWh are obtained by trapezoidal integration of the absolute MW levels between timeFrom and timeTo. Already accepted MWh for the same BMU and pair are subtracted using raw ISPStack.
  • Unclassified BMUs, including the register's OTHER category, are excluded from the BOD replacement pool so that an unidentified battery cannot be treated as non-battery.
  • The event sample follows the canonical panel's explicit BATTERY label. Known storage identities still labelled OTHER in the source register are outside the event sample pending a separate register correction.
  • No gas-price series is currently held in the repository, so the proposed gas-normalisation regression is not estimated in this report.
