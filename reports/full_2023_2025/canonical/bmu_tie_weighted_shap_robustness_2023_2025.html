Interpretation boundary

Mean |SHAP| is an importance allocation, while conditional signed SHAP is the average model contribution when that BMU×side dummy is active. Neither is a causal bid-price effect. Changes combine a genuinely reweighted RF fit with tie-weighted aggregation of the common SHAP sample. Active SHAP support remains sample-based; full marginal frequency is reported separately as raw rows and SP-equivalents.
